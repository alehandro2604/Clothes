Step 3 / 7 · Floors

Floor finishes

Patterned Maltese cement tiles run through the corridor and hall. The bedrooms and the street-side living room are in oak, the apartment kitchen in terrazzo, and the bathrooms in marble and porcelain.

Kitchens, bathrooms and furniture

Both kitchens have a fridge, induction hob, oven, extractor and sink. There's a bath and two showers across the bathrooms and ensuite, plus beds, wardrobes, sofas, dining sets and the gym kit.
